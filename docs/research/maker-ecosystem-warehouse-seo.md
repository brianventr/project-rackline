# Rackline — Maker Ecosystem & Warehouse SEO Keyword Research

**Product positioning:** WMS for makers who grow into manufacturers (Garage Mode → full warehouse). Not enterprise 3PL-first. Speak to founders, inventors, indie brands, and small manufacturers who *use* warehouses — often starting in a garage, unit, or shared shop.

**Competitive whitespace (from research):** Market splits into (A) maker/MRP tools that track materials/BOMs but are weak on bin-level WMS (Craftybase, Katana, Stockria, PartsBox, BOMIST), (B) ecommerce WMS/3PL tools that assume finished-goods fulfillment (ShipHero, Extensiv, Fulfillrite), and (C) mid-market ERP/WMS (Fishbowl, Cin7, MRPeasy). Rackline’s wedge: **true WMS execution (bays, FEFO, kits, pick/pack/ship, Shopify) in maker language, starting in Garage Mode.**
