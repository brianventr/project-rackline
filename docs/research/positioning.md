# Rackline Positioning One-Pager

## Wedge (locked)

> Warehouse software that starts with one aisle and stays with you.

Rackline is **warehouse execution for makers who manufacture** — Garage Mode on a bench and shelves, then a full floor on the same ledger. Not enterprise 3PL. Not MRP-only. Not shipping labels with a warehouse tax.

## ICP

| Segment | Profile | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Founders / inventors / indie brands selling on Shopify who assemble or kit what they sell | Spreadsheets break; first employee on the floor; crowdfunding wave arrives |
| **Secondary** | Small manufacturers outgrowing Craftybase/Katana’s weak bin WMS | Need directed pick, FEFO, locations, scan-to-move |
| **Tertiary** | Brands bringing fulfillment in-house from a 3PL or outgrowing ShipStation | Need real locations without ShipHero ACV |
| **Opportunistic** | Stocky refugees (app stopped Aug 31 2026) who also need a warehouse | PO/reorder/stocktake + bins |

## Anti-ICP (do not chase yet)

- Multi-client 3PLs needing Excalibur-class billing and EDI
- Enterprise DCs shopping Manhattan / SAP EWM
- Pure shipping-label shops with no desire to track locations
- Heavy job-shop MRP needing finite capacity Gantt as day-one

## Category line

**Rackline WMS** — Cloudflare-native warehouse management for makers who grow into manufacturers.

Always say **Rackline WMS** in titles and meta. Physical shelving brand rackline.com is unrelated; pursue `getrackline.com` / `rackline.app` before paid SEO.

## Do say / Don’t say

| Do say | Don’t say |
|---|---|
| Makers, founders, inventors, indie brands, small manufacturers | Enterprise 3PL, multi-client fulfillment-first |
| Garage Mode → full warehouse | “Replace your 3PL network” as first pitch |
| Kit the reward tier / assemble the BOM | NetSuite / SAP complexity |
| FEFO for coffee, beauty, supplements | Cannabis seed-to-sale / METRC (unless built) |
| Shopify is your storefront; Rackline is the floor | “All-in-one ERP” |
| In-house crowdfunding fulfillment | Compete head-on with Fulfillrite as a warehouse service |

## Proof points (product)

1. One inventory ledger — short stock is HTTP 409, not a silent negative
2. Floor map + scan-to-move (2D plan + 3D racks)
3. Garage Mode → Manufacturer on the same ledger
4. Shopify checkout → pick ticket → fulfillment back
5. Promise leave-by (does not reserve ATP)
6. Live day board, Runway stockout, FEFO/lots/serials, kits/as-built

## Competitive framing

| Vs | Frame |
|---|---|
| **Craftybase** | They own COGS recipes; we own the floor that executes them |
| **Katana** | They own production planning; we own bin-level WMS + Promise |
| **ShipHero** | They own high-volume DTC/3PL; we own makers who still manufacture |
| **ShipStation** | They own labels; we own locations, kits, and the leave-by |
| **Stocky** | They’re gone; we replace PO/reorder *and* give you a warehouse |
| **Enterprise WMS** | Start here; graduate there in a decade if you need to |

## Pricing posture (near-term)

Landing currently shows $0 self-serve tiers (Demo / Shop / Manufacturer). Monetization phase: Garage free with caps → paid Shop / Manufacturer. Do not fake enterprise pricing on the homepage until billing exists.

## Success signals

- Organic Tier-1 keyword impressions (`Shopify WMS`, `maker inventory software`, `Kickstarter fulfillment software`, `garage warehouse software`)
- LP → signup and Northwind demo loads
- Garage orgs that open Manufacturer mode
- Shopify-connected orgs; kit docs created; Promise ask usage
